# Heated Rivalry

> Two rival hockey players battling it out on the ice, hiding a connection that runs way deeper than the game itself.


## Quick Facts
* *Genre:* Sports Romance / Drama
* *Vibe:* Intense, slow-burn, and completely unforgettable
* *Setting:* Pro hockey leagues, arenas, and quiet hotel rooms on the road

## The Dynamic
* *On the Surface:* Total indifference and fierce competition every time they face off.
* *Behind Closed Doors:* A private tension that's been building for years.
* *Favorite Line:* "It was always about winning, until it became about everything else."

| :--- | :--- |
| *Chemistry* | Off-the-charts banter and high stakes|
